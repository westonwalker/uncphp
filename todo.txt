  3. Validation — simple rule-based validation for form input. required, email, min, max, unique etc. One of Laravel's most used features by far.

  Moderate value:

  8. Config system — a config/ folder with plain PHP files returning arrays, and a config('app.name') helper. Cleaner than stuffing everything in .env.
  9. Pagination — a simple helper that takes a total count and current page and returns page numbers, offsets, and limits. Very commonly needed.
